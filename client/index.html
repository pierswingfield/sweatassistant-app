About Sweat Assistant

Sweat Assistant — an unofficial companion for your gym, built for personal use.

What is this?

Sweat Assistant is an unofficial personal tool — not affiliated with or endorsed by your gym. It uses your own gym login to interact with the same API that powers that gym's own booking site.

Your login credentials are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored only on your private server. They are never transmitted to any third party.

Features

📅
Auto-Book

Schedules a booking to fire at the exact millisecond a class is released, giving you priority access. (Gyms differ: some release weekly, some per class — the countdown shows whichever applies.)

⬆️
Auto-Upgrade

Continuously monitors your booked class and upgrades you to a better spot if one becomes available.

⚡
Quick-Book

Books your preferred spot in any class in one click, using your saved seat preferences per studio.

🗺️
Preferred Spot Maps

Save your favourite seat positions per studio — used automatically by Quick-Book, Auto-Book, and Auto-Upgrade.

🔔
Push Notifications

Receive instant alerts on your device when Auto-Book or Auto-Upgrade takes action.

📆
Calendar Sync

Add your classes to Apple or Google Calendar. They stay in sync automatically — bookings, upgrades and cancellations update on their own.

Privacy & Data

All data is stored exclusively on your own server. Sweat Assistant stores your encrypted credentials, booking queue, spot preferences, and notification subscriptions — nothing else. You can delete all stored data at any time from the Settings tab.
