About Dibs

Dibs — your boutique fitness concierge.

Welcome Tour

Why?

Dibs gives you a one-stop-shop for finding and booking classes in your favuorute studios, all in one place:

  • Dibs remembers your favourite spots and bikes, and books them for you automatically.
  • Auto-Book secures your spot in class as soon as booking opens.
  • Auto-Upgrade checks for a better spot 24/7, and upgrades you automatically.
  • Calendar Sync keeps your Google and Apple Calendars up to date with classes as they are booked, changed or cancelled.
  • Flexible notifications help you plan, and avoid cancellation penalties.
  • Works with a growing list of boutique fitness studios.

Your login details are encrypted with your account, and stored securely in a private cloud. You can delete your Dibs account and stored data at any time from Settings.

Features

📅
Auto-Book

Automatically book at the exact millisecond a class is opened for your account, helping you secure the best spots.

⬆️
Auto-Upgrade

Monitors your booked class 24/7, and upgrades you to a better spot if one becomes available.

⚡
Quick-Book

Books your preferred spot in any class in one click, using your saved seat preferences per studio.

🗺️
Preferred Spot Maps

Save your favourite seat positions per studio. Set this up for each studio to power Quick-Book, Auto-Book, and Auto-Upgrade.

🔔
Push Notifications

Receive instant alerts on your device when you're Auto-Booked or Auto-Upgraded.

📆
Calendar Sync

Add your classes to Apple or Google Calendar. Bookings, upgrade, and cancellations update on their own.

Privacy & Data

Dibs uses your gym login credentials to do its magic, and they are saved within your account. Your data is encrypted and stored in a secure private cloud. You can delete your account, all your data and gym logins at any time from Settings.
