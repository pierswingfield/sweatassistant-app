## Decisions before publication

User-confirmed 7 November: Dibs is an unincorporated trading name operated from the United Kingdom; privacy contact is **privacy@dibs.fit**, intended to forward to **dibsadmin@piersj.com**. Cloudflare destination verification is complete; the exact forwarding rule is enabled and routing/DNS checks pass. End-to-end delivery is not yet tested. The controller/operator identity and service/contact address remain unresolved and are omitted from the user-facing drafts at the user’s request; this is a publication blocker requiring legal review. The Terms now choose England and Wales as a practical drafting assumption based on the London context, while preserving mandatory consumer protections and available courts; this choice needs legal review. These drafts remain unpublished.

1. **Identify the controller/operator:** legal name, individual/sole trader/company status, working support/privacy email, service address and jurisdiction. Repository content does not establish these. Do not turn a VAPID contact or personal developer identity into an asserted legal entity or invent an incorporated entity. Verify the requested mailbox works before publication. Decide whether any company/trader disclosures or ICO fee/registration apply.
2. **Provider and transfer inventory:** Google offsite backup disclosure was deliberately excluded from the user-facing drafts at the user’s request, although actual backup usage is known. Assess notice completeness and provider roles and terms before publication; do not infer that omission changes the backup operation. The live app and database are hosted on Oracle Cloud; Cloudflare supplies DNS, tunnel, edge/security, access and email services. Confirm the applicable contractual roles rather than assuming every service has the same role. Confirm Oracle region, Cloudflare features/logging and contractual roles, Google Drive crypt backup arrangement, push vendors, any support/email providers and gym/payment platforms. Record actual destinations, processor terms, transfer mechanism and access to safeguards. Hosting in London alone does not prove all processing stays in the UK. The notice names London hosting and Cloudflare’s global network, but cannot yet identify actual transfer destinations or mechanisms; it does not assert a UK-only service or that any transfer arrangement is in force.
3. **Retention/deletion:** approve a purpose-based schedule for dormant accounts, raw gym profiles, full class history, logs/support, Team/audit records, backup sets and exceptional legal holds. Implement review/purge where absent. The code's 30-day Team view is not a history-retention policy. Confirm deployed backup jobs, old snapshots and restore/deletion process before publishing 14/30-day claims. Account deletion cascades live account rows; it does not establish removal of all logs, audit data, caches or external copies.
4. **Lawful bases:** confirm contractual necessity for each feature and document legitimate-interest assessments for security and landing measurement. Contract wording cannot manufacture necessity. Review whether any actual provider profile fields/class types reveal special-category health information; fitness attendance is not automatically health data, but raw `profile_json` is broad and needs minimisation. Do not assume ordinary Article 6 bases settle Article 9 conditions if such data is present. Review whether booking automation warrants specific automated-decision information given credits/fees; the draft describes the actual consequence rather than asserting it has no significant effect.
5. **Tracking:** landing measurement hashes IP + user agent with a daily component and secret. That is pseudonymous, not proof of anonymity or exemption from PECR. Assess the actual storage/access mechanism and current statistical-purpose conditions; provide any necessary notice and simple free objection/control. The draft does not assert that a cookie banner is unnecessary.
6. **Gym terms and product scope:** review supported gyms' actual restrictions on credential sharing/automation. A user's warranty does not make prohibited API access lawful or eliminate operator responsibilities. Confirm service age/eligibility scope and whether Dibs is free/paid before adding age gates, pricing or a financial liability cap. The draft avoids a zero-liability cap and blanket indemnity. Obtain a UK consumer/data-protection legal review before relying on it.
7. **Publication and changes:** settle fields, review against actual deployed behavior and publish accessible linked pages before asking users to accept Terms. Give credential-use disclosure before gym linking and sharing information before joining Teams. Record accepted Terms version if adopting a contract acceptance flow; privacy information is a notice, not blanket consent. Make material changes prominent. Do not bury limits in About. This work does not authorise publication or new processing.

## Source guidance checked on 7 November 2026

- [UK GDPR, Chapter III](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/chapter/III): Article 13 requires controller identity and contact information; assess the omitted details before publication.
- [Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and GDPR](https://blogs.oracle.com/cloud-infrastructure/oracle-cloud-infrastructure-and-the-gdpr): describes Oracle’s processor role for customer service data, subject to the applicable services and terms.
- [Cloudflare metadata boundary FAQ](https://developers.cloudflare.com/data-localization/metadata-boundary/faq/): describes service metadata processing and distinguishes Cloudflare’s own purposes; assess each service and contract.
- [ICO: information a privacy notice must provide](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/what-privacy-information-should-we-provide/): operator identity, purposes/bases, recipients, transfers, retention and rights. This supports explicit missing fields rather than invented assurances.
- [ICO: legitimate interests](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/a-guide-to-lawful-basis/legitimate-interests/): identify, assess and balance the actual interest.
- [ICO: storage limitation](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/storage-limitation/): set justified retention/review; no universal time limit is prescribed.
- [ICO: transfer safeguards](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/appropriate-safeguards/what-are-the-rules-on-appropriate-safeguards/): identify the applicable mechanism before claiming protected international processing.
- [ICO: storage/access exceptions](https://cy.ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-the-exceptions/): statistical-purpose conditions include clear information and a simple free objection route. [DUAA overview](https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/what-we-do/legislation-we-cover/data-use-and-access-act-2025/the-data-use-and-access-act-2025-what-does-it-mean-for-organisations/) warns that guidance is changing; assess actual implementation, not the label “cookieless”.
- [CMA: fair consumer contracts, updated July 2026](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/writing-a-fair-contract-for-customers): material restrictions must be clear/prominent; broad exclusions may be unfair.
- [Consumer Rights Act 2015, s49](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/section/49), [s57](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/section/57), [s62](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/section/62), [s65](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/section/65): reasonable care/skill, limits on exclusions, fairness, and negligence causing death/injury. These are why the Terms retain statutory rights and operator responsibility for its own failures.
