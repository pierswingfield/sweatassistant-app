About Sweat Assistant

What is this?

Gym login credentials are encrypted and stored in a secure private cloud. Your Sweat Assistant account is separate from your gym logins. You can delete stored data at any time from Settings.

Features

📅
Auto-Book

Schedules a booking to fire at the exact millisecond a class is released, giving you priority access. (Gyms differ: some release weekly, some per class — the countdown shows whichever applies.)

⬆️
Auto-Upgrade

Continuously monitors your booked class and upgrades you to a better spot if one becomes available.

⚡
Quick-Book

Books your preferred spot in any class in one click, using your saved seat preferences per studio.

🗺️
Preferred Spot Maps

Save your favourite seat positions per studio — used automatically by Quick-Book, Auto-Book, and Auto-Upgrade.

🔔
Push Notifications

Receive instant alerts on your device when Auto-Book or Auto-Upgrade takes action.

📆
Calendar Sync

Add your classes to Apple or Google Calendar. They stay in sync automatically — bookings, upgrades and cancellations update on their own.

Privacy & Data

Gym login credentials are encrypted and stored in a secure private cloud. Your Sweat Assistant account is separate from your gym logins. You can delete all stored data at any time from Settings.
