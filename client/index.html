About Psycle App

An unofficial companion app for Psycle London, built for personal use.

What is this?

This is an unofficial personal tool — not affiliated with or endorsed by Psycle London or CodexFit. It uses your CodexFit credentials to interact with the same API that powers the Psycle website.

Your login credentials are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored only on your private server. They are never transmitted to any third party.

Features

📅
Auto-Book

Schedules a booking to fire at the exact millisecond a class is released (Monday 12PM), giving you priority access.

⬆️
Auto-Upgrade

Continuously monitors your booked class and upgrades you to a better spot if one becomes available.

⚡
Quick-Book

Books your preferred spot in any class in one click, using your saved seat preferences per studio.

🗺️
Preferred Spot Maps

Save your favourite seat positions per studio — used automatically by Quick-Book, Auto-Book, and Auto-Upgrade.

🔔
Push Notifications

Receive instant alerts on your device when Auto-Book or Auto-Upgrade takes action.

Privacy & Data

All data is stored exclusively on your own server. The app stores your encrypted credentials, booking queue, spot preferences, and notification subscriptions — nothing else. You can delete all stored data at any time from the Settings tab.
