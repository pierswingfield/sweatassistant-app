Stays out of your way

Works for you in the background.

Dibs books your classes, keeps you updated, and then gets out of the way.

Push alerts

A tap on the shoulder when a class is booked, when you've been upgraded, and before your free-cancel window closes.

Syncs with your calendar app.

Your Apple or Google Calendar is updated when you book, change, or cancel classes.

Credit warnings

Get a helpful reminder when your credits are low, ensuring your classes are booked reliably.

Favourites

Heart your favourite classes and instructors. They're one tap away across every gym.

Trust

Putting your data first.

Dibs securely uses your gym's credentials to book for you. We're transparent about how.

Encrypted at rest

Your gym passwords are stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and the key is kept outside the database. Your Dibs password is its own, separate from any gym's.

No gym affiliation

Dibs is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Psycle, JAB Boxing, AARMY or any other gym.

Leave any time

Unlink a gym, or delete your account and everything stored with it, whenever you like. Your gym membership itself is never touched.
